Summit Wood Species

Summit Custom Cabinetry

Ten wood species and cuts, plus two paint-grade constructions. Species sets the grain character of a door before any finish is applied — and because stain colors the wood rather than covering it, the species you choose changes how every stain in our palette will read.

12 Species & Materials
1. HDFPaint gradeSmooth and grainless. Doors, painted only.
2. Paint GradeWood frame, HDF panelWood rails and stiles with an HDF center panel. Painted only.

4. Clear Alder—Soft, even grain with minimal knots.
5. Knotty Alder—Open knots and natural character marks throughout.
6. Cherry—Fine, smooth grain. Darkens naturally with age.
7. Walnut—Rich chocolate tones, largely straight grain.

Shown unfinished. Samples are natural wood without stain or topcoat. A clear finish alone will deepen tone on most species, and stain will change it substantially.
Always review a physical sample before choosing.
A note on wood

Wood is a natural product. Grain pattern, density, mineral streaking and natural color vary from board to board and from tree to tree — two doors in the same species will never be identical, and this variation is a characteristic of the material, not a defect.

Cherry and walnut in particular continue to change color with light exposure over the first year. Rustic alder is selected for knots and character marks; the quantity and placement of those marks cannot be specified.

Five-piece wood doors are available in any species shown. HDF doors are painted only. Species availability and lead time may vary with market conditions.
